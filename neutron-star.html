5 · Numbers

Colours are false-colour blackbody: surface X-ray temperatures (~1–4 MK) are scaled ×1/400 into visible light, then shifted exactly as the physics shifts them (a blackbody at T is seen as a blackbody at gT). Time is slowed to a watchable rate, but every delay is computed at the true spin, so all phases are real. Literature: docs/NEUTRON_STAR_LITERATURE.md.
